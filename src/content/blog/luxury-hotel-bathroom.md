There’s something undeniably special about stepping into the bathroom of a beautiful five-star hotel. The soft lighting, luxurious materials, perfectly folded towels and calming atmosphere all contribute to a space that feels indulgent yet effortlessly elegant.

At Soraya Rose Studio, we believe your bathroom should offer that same sense of escape. Whether you’re planning a complete renovation or simply looking to elevate your existing space, here are our favourite ways to bring a little hotel luxury into your home.

## 01 Start With a Calming Colour Palette

The most beautiful hotel bathrooms often embrace a timeless, understated palette. Warm whites, soft taupes, natural stone and earthy neutrals create a tranquil environment that feels both sophisticated and welcoming. We particularly love combining limestone-inspired tiles with warm wood finishes and subtle metallic accents.

Avoid overly stark whites and harsh contrasts. Instead, opt for gentle tonal variations that create depth and warmth.

## 02 Invest in Beautiful Tiles

Tiles have the power to define the entire aesthetic of a bathroom. For a luxurious finish, consider large-format porcelain tiles with a natural stone appearance. Using the same tile across walls and floors can create a beautifully seamless effect, making even smaller bathrooms feel more spacious.

Travertine, limestone and marble-inspired finishes are particularly effective for achieving a timeless, hotel-inspired look.

> **Our tip** Choose a grout colour that closely matches your tiles to create a more refined and uninterrupted finish.

## 03 Layer Your Lighting

Lighting can completely transform the atmosphere of a bathroom. Rather than relying on bright overhead spotlights, incorporate several sources of softer, more flattering light. Wall lights positioned either side of a mirror provide beautiful, balanced illumination, while discreet LED lighting beneath a vanity can create a subtle floating effect.

For the ultimate hotel experience, consider dimmable lighting to create a relaxing atmosphere in the evening. Always ensure bathroom lighting is appropriately rated and installed for its location.

## 04 Create a Statement Vanity

A beautifully designed vanity can become the centrepiece of your bathroom. We love natural wood finishes, elegant stone countertops and understated cabinetry. Wall-mounted vanities are particularly effective in contemporary bathrooms, creating a feeling of space and lightness. For a truly luxurious finish, consider an integrated stone basin or a countertop basin paired with elegant wall-mounted taps.

## 05 Choose Timeless Brassware

The finish of your taps, shower fittings and accessories can have a significant impact on the overall design. Brushed brass adds warmth and sophistication, while polished nickel offers a beautifully timeless aesthetic. For a more contemporary look, brushed bronze can create an understated statement.

> **Our advice** Choose one principal metal finish and repeat it consistently throughout the space.

## 06 Make Your Shower an Experience

A generous walk-in shower is one of the defining features of a luxury hotel bathroom. Where space allows, consider a large rainfall showerhead alongside a separate handheld shower. Minimal glass screens, recessed shower niches and carefully considered drainage details all contribute to a beautifully streamlined appearance.

We particularly love fluted glass shower screens for their ability to introduce texture while maintaining a sense of elegance.

## 07 Introduce Hotel-Worthy Textiles

Never underestimate the impact of beautiful towels. Invest in generous, plush bath sheets in soft white, cream or warm neutral tones. Keep towels neatly folded or rolled, and consider adding a beautifully textured bath mat. Matching robes and high-quality bathroom textiles can make everyday routines feel that little bit more indulgent.

## 08 Style with Intention

The final touches are what transform a functional bathroom into a personal sanctuary. Decant everyday products into beautiful matching dispensers, display a favourite candle and introduce a simple tray to organise essentials. A small selection of carefully chosen accessories will create a far more luxurious impression than overcrowded shelves. Think understated elegance rather than excessive decoration.

## 09 Consider Underfloor Heating

Nothing says luxury quite like stepping onto a beautifully warm floor. Underfloor heating is a wonderful addition to a bathroom renovation, particularly when paired with stone or porcelain tiles. It provides a comfortable, even warmth while eliminating the need for additional visible heating elements in some spaces.

---

The secret to a truly luxurious bathroom lies in creating an experience rather than simply a beautiful room. It’s the warmth of the lighting, the softness of the towels, the quality of the materials and the sense of calm that welcomes you at the beginning and end of each day.
