Interiors that feel like home

Beautifully considered living

The studio brings together a shared passion for exceptional design, refined aesthetics and meticulous attention to detail. With a wealth of experience spanning luxury residential developments, architectural interior design and high-end hospitality, we offer a personal and collaborative approach to every project.

We believe that truly beautiful interiors should be more than simply visually captivating. They should reflect the individuality of those who inhabit them, balancing effortless sophistication with comfort, practicality and a sense of belonging.

Our signature aesthetic

Timeless, enduring elegance

From complete home transformations and extensive renovations to individual room designs, our approach is tailored to each client’s vision, lifestyle and aspirations.

Our signature aesthetic embraces timeless elegance, natural materials, rich textures and carefully curated details. Whether creating a contemporary retreat or reimagining a traditional residence, we strive to achieve interiors that feel effortlessly luxurious, harmonious and enduring.

At Soraya Rose Studio, we pride ourselves on building lasting relationships with our clients, guiding them through the design journey with creativity, expertise and an unwavering commitment to quality.
